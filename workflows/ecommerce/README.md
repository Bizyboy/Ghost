# E-commerce Automation

## Overview
Build and scale an e-commerce business using Shopify, Amazon, and automation tools for product sourcing, marketing, and fulfillment.

## Business Models

### 1. Private Label
- Source generic products
- Add your brand
- Sell at premium
- **Profit margin**: 30-50%
- **Startup**: $2,000-10,000

### 2. Wholesale
- Buy bulk from manufacturers
- Sell at retail price
- **Profit margin**: 20-40%
- **Startup**: $1,000-5,000

### 3. Retail Arbitrage
- Buy discounted retail items
- Resell for profit
- **Profit margin**: 15-30%
- **Startup**: $500-2,000

### 4. Amazon FBA
- Send inventory to Amazon
- They handle fulfillment
- **Fees**: 15% + fulfillment
- **Startup**: $3,000-10,000

## Platform Comparison

### Amazon
- **Pros**: Huge traffic, FBA, trust
- **Cons**: High competition, fees 15-30%
- **Best for**: Scale and automation

### Shopify
- **Pros**: Your brand, control, lower fees
- **Cons**: Need to drive traffic
- **Best for**: Building a brand

### eBay
- **Pros**: Easy to start, auction format
- **Cons**: Lower prices, fees 10-15%
- **Best for**: Unique/used items

## Product Research

### What to Look For
✅ High demand (>100 sales/month)
✅ Low competition (<100 reviews)
✅ Good margins (30%+ profit)
✅ Small and light (low shipping)
✅ Not seasonal
✅ Not fragile
