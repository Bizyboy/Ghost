## Pricing Strategy

### Value-Based Pricing
Don't price based on time spent. Price based on value delivered.

**Examples:**
- Budget tracker template: Saves $500/year → Price at $29
- Resume template: Gets $60k job → Price at $19
- Business plan template: Worth $500 consultant → Price at $49

### Price Ranges by Product Type
- **Simple templates**: $5-15
- **Complex templates**: $20-50
- **Template bundles**: $30-100
- **E-books**: $10-50
- **Video courses**: $50-500
- **Masterminds**: $500-5,000

## Marketing Strategy

### Pinterest (Best for Digital Products)
- 80% of users come to buy
- Create pins for each product
- SEO-optimize pin descriptions
- Post 10-20 pins daily
- Link to product pages

### Instagram
- Showcase product mockups
- Show before/after results
- Share customer testimonials
- Use relevant hashtags
- Link in bio

### Email Marketing
- Build list with free lead magnet
- Send weekly value emails
- Promote products naturally
- Segment by interest
- Automate sequences

### SEO & Content
- Write blog posts solving problems
- Mention products in content
- Optimize for search
- Build backlinks
- Guest post on relevant sites

### Paid Ads
- Start with Pinterest ads ($5-10/day)
- Facebook/Instagram for specific niches
- Google Ads for high-intent keywords
- Track ROAS carefully

## Automation Workflow

### Product Creation (ChatGPT + Canva)
```
1. ChatGPT: Generate product ideas
2. ChatGPT: Create content/copy
3. Canva: Design templates
4. Export: Package deliverables
5. Upload: To selling platform
```

### Listing Creation (n8n)
```
1. Trigger: New product ready
2. ChatGPT: Generate title + description
3. Auto-create: Gumroad product
4. Auto-create: Etsy listing
5. Social: Auto-post announcement
6. Email: Notify subscribers
```

### Sales & Delivery (Automated)
```
1. Customer purchases
2. Payment processed automatically
3. Product delivered instantly
4. Welcome email sent
5. Upsell sequence triggered
6. Review request (7 days later)
```
