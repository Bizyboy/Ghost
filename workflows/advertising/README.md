# Digital Advertising Automation

## Overview
Automated advertising campaigns using AI for ad creation, platform APIs for deployment, and smart optimization for maximum ROI.

## Platform Comparison

| Platform | Best For | Avg CPC | Conv Rate | Min Budget |
|----------|----------|---------|-----------|------------|
| Google Ads | High-intent search | $1-5 | 2-5% | $30/day |
| Facebook Ads | Interest targeting | $0.50-2 | 1-3% | $20/day |
| Instagram Ads | Visual products | $0.50-1.50 | 1-2% | $15/day |
| TikTok Ads | Viral content | $0.50-1 | 2-4% | $20/day |
| YouTube Ads | Video content | $0.10-0.30 | 1-2% | $10/day |

## Success Metrics

### Key Performance Indicators
- **ROAS**: Return on Ad Spend (target: 3-5x)
- **CPA**: Cost Per Acquisition (track by channel)
- **CTR**: Click-Through Rate (target: 2-5%)
- **Conversion Rate**: Visitors to customers (target: 2-5%)
- **LTV**: Lifetime Value (maximize this)
