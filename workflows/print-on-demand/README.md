# Print on Demand Automation

## Overview
Zero-inventory business model selling custom-designed products. Designs are printed only when orders are placed.

## Best Print-on-Demand Platforms

### 1. Printful
- **Products**: 300+ items
- **Quality**: Premium
- **Shipping**: Worldwide
- **Integration**: Excellent
- **Base Cost**: T-shirt ~$10

### 2. Printify
- **Products**: 800+ items
- **Quality**: Good (multiple suppliers)
- **Shipping**: Worldwide
- **Cost**: Cheaper than Printful
- **Base Cost**: T-shirt ~$7

### 3. Redbubble
- **Type**: Marketplace
- **Effort**: Upload and forget
- **Commission**: 20% base
- **Competition**: High
- **Best for**: Testing designs

### 4. Teespring (Spring)
- **Type**: Platform
- **Marketing**: Built-in tools
- **Commission**: High margins
- **Best for**: Influencers

## Design Strategy

### What Sells
✅ Text-based designs (70% of sales)
✅ Simple and bold
✅ Relatable humor
✅ Inspirational quotes
✅ Niche-specific
✅ High contrast colors

### What Doesn't Sell
❌ Complex designs
❌ Too many colors
❌ Low resolution
❌ Generic messages
❌ Copyrighted content

## Automation Setup

### Design Creation (Daily)
1. ChatGPT generates 10 design ideas
2. Create in Canva (2-4 hours)
3. Upload to Printful
4. Generate mockups automatically
5. Save organized by niche

### Listing Creation (n8n)
1. Trigger: New design uploaded
2. ChatGPT generates title/description
3. Auto-create Etsy listing
4. Auto-create Shopify product
5. Post to social media

### Order Fulfillment (100% Auto)
1. Customer orders from Etsy/Shopify
2. Order sent to Printful automatically
3. Printful prints and ships
4. Customer gets tracking
5. You get paid (zero work)

## Common Mistakes

❌ Copying other designs (copyright issues)
✅ Create original designs or use commercial licenses

❌ Too complicated designs
✅ Keep it simple and bold

❌ Not testing enough
✅ Create volume, find winners

❌ Poor quality mockups
✅ Use high-quality product photos

❌ Giving up too soon
✅ Takes 3-6 months to gain traction

## Success Tips

1. **Volume is key** - Create 100+ designs
